How it works

Three steps, zero configuration.

01

ciotx scan .

The CLI reads your source files locally. Secrets, lock files, and binaries are excluded automatically. Nothing is stored or logged on your machine.

02

Three-pass AI analysis

Your code is analyzed on our servers. Discovery finds candidates. Verification re-anchors each to real source lines. A skeptical second pass rejects false positives.

03

Report delivered

Two files appear in your directory: a compliance-grade PDF report and a machine-readable JSON file. Every finding includes severity, CWE, exact line numbers, evidence, and remediation guidance.
